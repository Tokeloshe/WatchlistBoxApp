# Dedication

**WatchlistBox** is for my brother,

## George Thomas Honiball

whose deep appreciation of film and television inspired this project.

A good film deserves a good seat and a box that just works: no desktop in the way, no
menus to fight, the picture on the screen and the remote in your hand. That is what this
program tries to be, and it is George's taste that set the bar.

---

WatchlistBox is built by **James Honiball**. It is a Honiball brothers app.
