## 3. Support without spending a cent

These matter more than you'd think:

- **Star the repository.** It is how other people find the project.
- **Report what breaks.** An issue with your Windows or macOS version and the diagnostics zip
  from the error card is worth an afternoon of guessing.
- **Tell one person** who has a spare PC or Mac and a television.
- **Say what you would change.** The things that annoy you daily are the next release.

Thank you.
